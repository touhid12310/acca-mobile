# AccountE — 10 promo video briefs

**Common spec (all ten)**
- 56.0 s total: content 0–50.4 s, shared end card 50.4–56 s (cuts land on the music grid: 2.8 s = one scene beat).
- 1920×1080 (16:9 master) and 1080×1920 (9:16, re-composed, not cropped). 30 fps, H.264 High, AAC 48 kHz.
- Arc: hook ≤ 3 s → message → support → CTA. A new beat every 2.8 s (18 beats + end card), and the frame is always moving.
- Fonts: **Urbanist** in every video, plus at most one partner font (listed per video).
- Captions: burned in, white on 82% ink pill (AA). Positioned clear of platform UI: 16:9 bottom 58 px; 9:16 bottom 470 px. Hidden on the end card, which states the same words on screen.
- Voice-over: Kokoro-82M neural TTS (Apache 2.0), natural English. One voice per video.
- Music and SFX: original compositions synthesized for this project, so they are royalty-free. Mixed to −16 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP, with VO ducking.
- **End card (identical in all ten):** AccountE wordmark · "Your AI bookkeeper in your pocket" · "Free forever plan · No credit card" · official Google Play badge · accounte.com. VO: "AccountE. Your AI bookkeeper in your pocket. Start free on Google Play."
- Sample data is fictional but realistic: persona "Alex", neutral merchants (Green Leaf Market, Corner Café, Northwind Hosting…) and accounts (Main Checking, Savings, Everyday Card). No real brands, people, stats, prices or testimonials.
